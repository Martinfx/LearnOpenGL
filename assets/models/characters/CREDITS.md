# Character models

## robot.glb

"RobotExpressive" by [Tomás Laulhé (Quaternius)](https://quaternius.com/),
license **CC0 1.0** (public domain). glTF conversion and facial morph targets by
[Don McCurdy](https://donmccurdy.com/). Taken unmodified from the three.js
examples (`examples/models/gltf/RobotExpressive/RobotExpressive.glb`).

Animation clips: Dance, Death, Idle, Jump, No, Punch, Running, Sitting,
Standing, ThumbsUp, Walking, WalkJump, Wave, Yes.
