CC-BY 4.0 items: "This work is based on <title> by <author>, licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)". Converted to metres, textures resized, some decimated.

All converted, weathered and cut into sections for this project (`source/`).

`sign_gantry_18m.glb` and everything in `rail/` were generated for this project (`rail/source/railgen.py`, CC0) with Poly Haven CC0 textures from `assets/textures/pbr`. Vegetation credits are in `vegetation/CREDITS.md`.

Everything in `gen/` (vehicles, rolling stock, enterable buildings, cooling towers, pipe bridges, bridge, river,
skyline towers, low-poly vegetation, debris, signs) is generated for this project by `source/build_zone.py`
(`source/zonegen/`), CC0. Its shared textures in `textures/` are 1K repacks of Poly Haven CC0 sets from
`assets/textures/pbr` (albedo, OpenGL normal, ORM = occlusion/roughness/metallic), plus generated birch bark,
panel facade and Cyrillic sign textures (text set in DejaVu Sans, Bitstream Vera license; place names are fictional).

Everything in `street/` (pre-war brick and plaster tenements with balconies, cornices, pipes and leaks, kerb runs, sagging
cables) is generated for the overgrown street level by `assets/levels/source/make_overgrown_street.py --models`
(`source/zonegen/tenement.py`, `fixtures.py`), CC0. Its facade textures `textures/st_*` are 1K repacks of Poly Haven CC0
sets (red_brick_03, red_brick_plaster_patch_02, rough_plaster_brick, worn_cracked_plaster, peeling_painted_wall,
dark_brick_wall, slumped_mortar_brick, worn_brick_wall, beige_wall_002, rusted_shutter, rusty_painted_metal,
cracked_concrete). The shop signs over the shop fronts (`textures/signs_albedo.png`: ГАСТРОНОМ, ХЛЕБ, АПТЕКА...) are
generated by `fixtures.make_sign_atlas` (lettering in DejaVu Sans Bold, Bitstream Vera license), CC0.

The old-town street in `postwar/` is based on "Postwar City - Exterior Scene" by Aurélien Martel (CC-BY 4.0); see `postwar/CREDITS.md`.
