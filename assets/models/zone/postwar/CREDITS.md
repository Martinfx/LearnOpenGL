# Postwar street (old town of `hospital_street.level`)

`postwar_street.glb` and `postwar_street_alpha.glb` are converted with
`assets/models/zone/source/postwar_convert.py`: the sky and backdrop walls are dropped,
textures are capped at 2048 px (1024 px for alpha-cut foliage/fences), and the
alpha-cut materials are split into `postwar_street_alpha.glb`.

This work is based on "Postwar City - Exterior Scene"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/postwar-city-exterior-scene-30b694d1a4074855a1116a15a0f75731)
by Aurélien Martel (https://sketchfab.com/aurelien_martel) licensed under CC-BY-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The original scene uses textures from Textures.com, which are included in the
model as distributed on Sketchfab.
