# Credits: Dead City street props

All models in this folder were made for this project by `source/dc_props.py` (procedural Blender geometry) and are
released under **CC0 1.0**. Sign faces are drawn procedurally in `source/textures.py` with DejaVu Sans Bold
(Bitstream Vera licence, see `assets/fonts/`). They show generic US road sign designs (public MUTCD shapes, no branding),
and the street names (MAIN ST, ELM AVE) are generic.

Textures are derived from these **CC0** Poly Haven scans (https://polyhaven.com, credits also in `assets/textures/pbr/CREDITS.md`):

| Used for | Poly Haven asset |
|---|---|
| rust on drums, dumpsters, containers, signals, rebar | `rust_coarse_01`, `rusty_painted_metal` (paint layer normals) |
| corrugated container panels | `rusty_corrugated_iron` |
| brick ruins | `red_brick_03`, `dark_brick_wall`, `red_brick_plaster_patch_02` |
| concrete, curbs, Jersey barriers, slabs | `concrete_wall_007`, `cracked_concrete` |
| rubble | `concrete_debris` |
| sidewalk slabs | `concrete_pavement` |
| road decals | `road_damaged` |

`tyre_*` models reuse `assets/models/props/old_tyre` (Poly Haven "Old Tyre", CC0), decimated.
Paint colours, plastics, reflective sheeting and lens materials are procedural.
