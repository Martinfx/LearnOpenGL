# Sounds

All sounds in this folder are original placeholders synthesized from noise and
oscillators by [`../tools/make_sounds.py`](../tools/make_sounds.py) (CC0, no
recordings). Regenerate with `python3 assets/tools/make_sounds.py`.

`.sound` files group the variations of one effect with its playback settings
(volume, random pitch, 3D distance range, loop, stream, mixer group); edit them
in the editor's sound editor.
